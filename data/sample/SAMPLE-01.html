REQUIREMENTS FOR REGISTRATION OF GARDEN WATER PUMPS

Issued by the Examplia Standards Authority (ESA). Synthetic sample document for testing.

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 This document sets out how garden water pumps are registered before they are sold in Examplia.

2. PURPOSE

2.1 The purpose of this document is to protect users from electric shock and to make sure that pumps are labelled correctly.

3. SCOPE

3.1 This document applies to the following products:

3.1.1 Electric garden water pumps with a rated voltage between 100 and 250 volts alternating current.

3.1.2 Pumps with a rated current of not more than 10 ampere.

3.2 This document shall not apply to pumps covered by ESA Standard 44-9.

4. REFERENCES

4.1 ESA Standard 44-1:2019, Safety of household and similar pumps.

4.2 Water Safety Law No. 7 of 2020.

5. DEFINITION OF TERMS

5.1 Authority: the Examplia Standards Authority (ESA), set up under Water Safety Law No. 7 of 2020.

5.2 Product: any pump within the scope of clause 3.1.

5.3 Supplier: the manufacturer or importer that places a product on the market in Examplia.

5.4 Registration Certificate: the certificate issued by the Authority for a registered product.

5.5 Test Report: a report from a laboratory recognised by the Authority.

6. REQUIREMENTS

6.1 The supplier shall register each product model with the Authority before the product is sold.

6.2 Each product shall carry a label that shows the rated voltage and the name of the supplier.

6.3 The supplier should keep a copy of each test report for five years.

7. PROCEDURE

7.1 The supplier shall submit the application form and the test report to the Authority.

7.2 The Authority may ask for up to two samples of the product.

8. VALIDITY

8.1 A Registration Certificate shall be valid for two years.

8.2 The supplier shall apply for renewal one month before the Registration Certificate expires.

9. ENFORCEMENT

9.1 The Authority reserves the right to suspend a registration if a product is found to be unsafe.

9.2 A supplier that sells a product without registration shall pay a fine of USD 5,000.

9.3 The steps for registration are given in clause 7 of this document.
